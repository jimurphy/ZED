ZED 1.0.0 RC1

The VST3 plug-in is installed for all users in Common Files\VST3\ZED.vst3.
Restart your DAW. If ZED does not appear, rescan VST3 plug-ins in the DAW settings.
Use a native x64 DAW on Windows 10 or Windows 11.

To uninstall, close all DAWs and remove ZED through Windows Installed Apps
(Apps & features on Windows 10).
